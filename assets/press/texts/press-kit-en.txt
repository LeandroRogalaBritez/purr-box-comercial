Purr Box — PRESS KIT

Short description
Purr Box is a pixel-art idle and incremental cat game for Windows. Collect companions, earn purrs, upgrade your cozy room and bring your cats onto your desktop in desktop pet mode.

About the game
A simple cardboard box is the beginning of a home full of cats. In Purr Box, you earn purrs through clicks and your cats’ production, welcome new companions and invest in upgrades to grow your collection. Between discoveries, fulfill your cats’ requests, complete diary tasks and save moments in your memory album. The game also includes a Memory Game, Ginger’s Run, Cat Minesweeper and a Pomodoro timer for focus and rest. In desktop pet mode, your cats keep you company along a strip on your desktop, with game features accessible through a compact menu.
